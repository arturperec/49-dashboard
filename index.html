How these are counted
  • A day is the time from one daily check to the next (the stats job runs every morning), shown with the check's date. The last day, marked "so far", is from the last morning check until now.
  • Active player: a tester who opened 49 at least once that day, from Apple's TestFlight session counts. Apple's counts can arrive a day late, so recent days may rise later.
  • WAU / MAU: different players active in the last 7 / 30 days. Stickiness is daily players ÷ weekly players: how many days of the week a typical player comes back (100% = every day).
  • New players are active for the first time since tracking began; returning were active the day before too; back were away and came back; left were active the day before but not that day.
  • Churn rate: of everyone who has ever played, the share who haven't played for the chosen number of days. Players from before tracking began who never played since count from the first check.
  • Retention, classic: of the players who started on a day, the share who played again exactly N days later. Rolling: the share who played N or more days later (steadier with few players).
  • Moving average: the mean of the last N days, including that day. Trend: the least-squares slope, change per day. Std dev: how much a day usually varies from the mean.
  • Crashes and installs come per build, so they always include the team.
