How these are counted
  • A day is the time from one daily check to the next. The stats job runs every morning, though GitHub often starts it a few hours late, so each day is named after the date its play mostly falls on: the day the earlier check was taken. Point at a day in a chart to see exactly when it started and ended (Warsaw time). The last day, marked "so far", runs from the last check until now.
  • Active player: a tester who opened 49 at least once that day, from Apple's TestFlight session counts. Apple's counts can arrive a day late, so recent days may rise later.
  • WAU / MAU: different players active in the last 7 / 30 days. Stickiness is daily players ÷ weekly players: how many days of the week a typical player comes back (100% = every day).
  • New players are active for the first time since tracking began; returning were active the day before too; back were away and came back; left were active the day before but not that day.
  • Churn rate: of everyone who has ever played, the share who haven't played for the chosen number of days. Players from before tracking began who never played since count from the first check.
  • Retention, classic: of the players who started on a day, the share who played again exactly N days later. Rolling: the share who played N or more days later (steadier with few players).
  • Moving average: the mean of the last N days, including that day. Trend: the least-squares slope, change per day. Std dev: how much a day usually varies from the mean.
  • Came from: Poster is the players who joined through the posters' own TestFlight link, which the QR code has opened since 4 November 2026. Website is everyone else, including anyone who scanned a poster before then (it opened the website's link). The testers, installs and crashes charts and In the game always show everyone: Apple counts those per build, and the app can't tell which link a player used.
  • Crashes and installs come per build, so they always include the team.
  • In the game comes from 49's own statistics in iCloud (from build 38): one record a day per phone, from players who have statistics on and iCloud signed in. It counts App Store players too, and can't tell the team apart or which link a player came from, so "Came from" and the team box don't change it. A player-day is one player on one day. The three in-game tiles (Daily take-up, hints, newest build) show the last day that is over: phones add to today's record all day.
  • Retention by start week groups players by the Monday-to-Sunday week of their first day (TestFlight sessions), and a cell counts only the players whose day N has already come.
