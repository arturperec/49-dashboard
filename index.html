How these are counted
  • A day is the time from one daily check to the next. The stats job runs every morning, though GitHub often starts it a few hours late, so each day is named after the date its play mostly falls on: the day the earlier check was taken. Point at a day in a chart to see exactly when it started and ended (Warsaw time). The last day, marked "so far", runs from the last check until now.
  • Two sources of players. The tiles, the Summary and every card without a TestFlight only badge count everyone from 49's own statistics (App Store and TestFlight, players with statistics on and iCloud signed in, since build 38 on 3 November). Players already there on 3 November count as from before: they have no start day, so retention starts with the players who began on 4 November. The TestFlight testers section, with its tiles and Summary lines, shows only with Show TestFlight testers ticked. It counts only TestFlight's testers, from Apple's daily TestFlight checks: since the App Store launch on 11 November most players aren't in it. Came from changes only that section. The team box covers both: your phone is left out of the game's statistics too, recognized by its iCloud account.
  • Phones: 49's own statistics come from iPhones (in iCloud, with iCloud signed in) and from Android phones (in Firebase, with Play Games signed in, from Android version 0.14). Both save the same daily record, so every in-game card can show both or either. TestFlight's and the App Store's numbers are iPhone only, so Android leaves them out. Builds are kept apart: "Android 14" is Android's version 0.14, a plain number is an iPhone build. Android days from 49 Dev count as the team's.
  • Active player: a tester who opened 49 at least once that day, from Apple's TestFlight session counts. Apple's counts can arrive a day late, so recent days may rise later.
  • WAU / MAU: different players active in the last 7 / 30 days. Stickiness is daily players ÷ weekly players: how many days of the week a typical player comes back (100% = every day).
  • New players are active for the first time since tracking began; returning were active the day before too; back were away and came back; left were active the day before but not that day.
  • Churn rate: of everyone who has ever played, the share who haven't played for the chosen number of days. Players from before tracking began who never played since count from the first check.
  • Retention, classic: of the players who started on a day, the share who played again exactly N days later. Rolling: the share who played N or more days later (steadier with few players).
  • Moving average: the mean of the last N days, including that day. Trend: the least-squares slope, change per day. Std dev: how much a day usually varies from the mean.
  • Came from: where players came from: the Website, the Poster, the App Store itself (search and browsing), or Other. It changes two kinds of numbers. TestFlight testers, by the link they joined with: the poster's own TestFlight link (from 4 November 2026), the website's (any other public link, so anyone who scanned a poster before 4 November too), or an email invitation (Other). Nobody joins TestFlight from the App Store, so App Store leaves TestFlight out. App Store downloads (First downloads by where they came from), from Apple's analytics reports, a day or two late: the poster's QR code opens the App Store with its own tag (ct=poster), the website is the visits Apple names as 49game.github.io, and Other is links in apps (Messenger and the like), other websites and web visits Apple doesn't name (it names a website only when enough came from it that day). The players in the game, retention and everything else from 49's own statistics count everyone whatever Came from says: the app can't tell which link a player used.
  • Crashes and installs come per build, so they always include the team.
  • In the game comes from 49's own statistics in iCloud (from build 38): one record a day per phone, from players who have statistics on and iCloud signed in. It counts App Store players too, and can't tell which link a player came from, so "Came from" doesn't change it. The team's phones are recognized by their iCloud account and left out unless the team box is ticked. A player-day is one player on one day. The three in-game tiles (Daily take-up, hints, newest build) show the last day that is over: phones add to today's record all day.
  • Ad earnings stay hidden until Show earnings is tapped, on every opening, and the same button hides them again. They are AdMob's estimates (the final amount is settled at the end of each month), read every morning with the AdMob API. Per player (ARPU) divides them by the players in the game: one day's players for a day (ARPDAU), the different players over a longer stretch. Phones without statistics earn but aren't counted, so per player reads a little high. AdMob can't tell the team's phones apart, so the team box only changes the players.
  • Retention by start week groups players by the Monday-to-Sunday week of their first day (TestFlight sessions), and a cell counts only the players whose day N has already come.
